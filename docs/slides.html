Product Architecture

The Four Operational Answers

Windwise does not invent prices or fake demand models. It answers four specific operational questions for the procurement trader.

1. What did the market know at gate?
Zero Look-Ahead Snapshot Selection
Pulls the latest complete WINDFOR snapshot published strictly before market gate (e.g. 09:30 snapshot for 09:50 N2EX cut-off). Fallbacks logged transparently.
2. What is the calibrated expectation?
Walk-Forward Ratio Calibration
k = Σactual / Σforecast, estimated only from complete days up to D-2 (min 7 days). Cuts N2EX MAE by 40.5% (2,123 MW → 1,263 MW).
3. How low could wind plausibly go?
Empirical Leave-One-Day-Out Residuals
P10/P20/min residual bands. Realised coverage: 85.1% (80% floor), 88.9% (90% floor), 97.3% (stress). Never promises a false 95% guarantee.
4. How much cover does the client hold?
Firm Thermal Cover Book
Cover = share × max(0, forecast − firm wind). Instant slider re-computation on client + tested server-side summary parity.

Statistical Methodology

Calibration & Leave-One-Day-Out Bands

Walk-forward lag protects against look-ahead bias; leave-one-day-out ensures empirical bands are not artificially narrowed by in-sample overfit.

Walk-Forward Lagged Ratio (k)

Formula: k = Σ actual / Σ forecast

Lag Rule: Only complete delivery days up to D-2 are included (yesterday D-1 is still ongoing at gate time).

Warm-Up: Minimum 7 days required. Jan 1–8 marked provisional; Jan 9 unlocks calibration.

Leave-One-Day-Out Residuals (LODO)

Out-of-sample: Each historical day i is evaluated using k-i computed without day i.

Residuals: e = actual − (k-i × forecast)

Quantiles: Central 80% (P10–P90), Central 95% (P2.5–P97.5), Firm-90 floor (P10 residual), Stress (min residual).

Speaker Cue (Part 2: 1:25–2:20)
"Calibration is one parameter: k equals total actual over total forecast, over complete delivery days at or before D-2. Bands come from leave-one-day-out residuals. Each historical day is scored with a k estimated without that day, so the band is not narrowed by in-sample fitting."

Project Retrospective

Honest Limitations & Next Steps

Transparent engineering: recognizing the boundaries of the prototype and outlining a clear path to enterprise production.

Known Limitations

1. Single Month Scope: One winter month does not capture seasonal wind variance (spring calms, summer low-demand).

2. Single-Ratio Constraint: A constant ratio assumes stable bias across wind speeds, which fails during deep calm events.

3. Empirical Bands: Historical residuals provide empirical confidence, not statistical guarantees.

Production Roadmap

1. Automated Live Ingestion: Connect Elexon BMRS streaming API with incremental SQLite updates.

2. Multi-Season Non-Linear Calibration: Wind-speed dependent piecewise quantile calibration.

3. Client Demand Integration: Pair national wind cover with supplier-specific half-hourly customer demand profiles.

Speaker Cue (Part 2: 4:30–4:50)
"The limits are real. One month is not a season. A single ratio assumes a stable bias, and January violates that in the tail. Windwise is a decision-support prototype built to make those limits visible. The next step would be live ingestion and multi-season calibration, not an overly complex model on one month of data."
